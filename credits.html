Credits, source and data

San Juan Spin-off: EUC Thrills

El Viejo San Juan: the Old San Juan world by VibezZzCoder. San Juan source repository. Thanks to Mr.doob and Kamil for the NYC and SF foundations, the EUC Thrills riders and contributors, and the mappers of Puerto Rico.

Code, art and sound

World and riding code: MIT. Assets from EUC Thrills by VibezZzCoder — CC BY 4.0 with exceptions. See the rider and asset notices and individual sound credits. Real identities, contributed recordings and marks have separate terms.

City life adapts vehicle models and synthesized sound from the EUC Thrills New York edition, and traffic, pedestrian figures and reactions, and actor collisions from the San Francisco edition. Thanks to VibezZzCoder, Mr.doob and Kamil for the MIT code. See the file-by-file credits. San Juan's liveries, parked rows, wardrobe and routes were authored for this city; the liveries carry no real maker, company or agency marks.

Changing rider (preparing a rider and its crash voice together, then swapping both at once) adapts code from the EUC Thrills San Francisco edition, MIT, by VibezZzCoder. See the file-by-file credits. The picker's stamps and postcard were designed for San Juan. The eleven rider portraits on them are by VibezZzCoder, from the original EUC Thrills v1.1.0 rider chooser, unchanged (generated into js/euc/ui/riderPortraits.js; generator record); three carry those riders' own supplied marks. The marks and the real riders' identities keep their separate terms.

Skatepark del Muelle is a fictional park. Its geometry kit adapts the skatepark kit of the EUC Thrills San Francisco edition (with its New York inheritance), and its profiles and shapes follow the Foundry Yard of the original EUC Thrills, MIT, by VibezZzCoder. See the file-by-file credits. Its layout, paint, lettering and flag panel were made for San Juan.

Officer Dorkins, the fictional officer on his hover wheel from the original EUC Thrills, chases riders here with pursuit, paddle and booking code adapted from the EUC Thrills San Francisco edition (with its New York inheritance), MIT, by VibezZzCoder. See the file-by-file credits. His routes through the old city, the Cuartelillo del Monociclo and its desk were made for San Juan; the Cuartelillo is invented, uses La Princesa only as a backdrop, and carries no real force's name, crest or insignia.

Three.js: MIT. Inter, Overpass and Fraunces (the Fraunces Project Authors): SIL Open Font License 1.1. The menus, the loading poster, the badge and the tile border are original drawings for this game (MIT). The rider portraits were drawn for the original EUC Thrills and are reused here under their separate terms (see above).

Geography and downloads

El Bowl in La Perla is a real skate bowl and pool, built by hand in 2006 by Chemi Rosado-Seijo with Roberto “Boly” Cortez, Luiggi Ramos, residents and visiting skaters; the game's version is an approximation of its shape and its paintwork is original.
